## PINN Examples

1. `01_steady_heat_linear.ipynb`  
   Steady 1D heat conduction with Dirichlet boundary conditions.

2. `02_pinn_1d_poisson_source.ipynb`  
   1D Poisson equation with a non-zero source term.

3. `03_pinn_1d_heat_generation_robin.ipynb`  
   Steady heat conduction with internal heat generation and a convective Robin boundary condition.

4. `04_pinn_1d_transient_heat.ipynb`  
   Transient 1D heat conduction with space-time collocation, initial and boundary conditions, and analytical error validation.
